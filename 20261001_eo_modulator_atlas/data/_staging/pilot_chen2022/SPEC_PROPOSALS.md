# SPEC proposals from the chen2022 pilot (T-rail CLTW, G-S-G, undercut Si)

Each item: gap, what chen2022 needs, closest valid form used in `sims/chen2022/config.yaml`.

1. Mirror plane and symmetry type. `symmetry` only has `none | x_mirror_odd` and the origin must be on the optical axis. This device is G-S-G: potential is mirror-EVEN about the signal centre, and the mirror plane is not at the optical axis (right waveguide axis at +55.4 um from it). Propose `symmetry: x_mirror_even` plus `mirror_x_um: <value>` (mirror plane position in the config frame; default 0). Used: `symmetry: none`, full domain with both arms (about 490 um wide), optical window on the right arm.
2. Drive polarity semantics. `line.differential` (Vpi quoted for V+ - V-) does not describe a single-ended G-S-G drive of a push-pull MZM. Propose `line.drive: single_ended_gsg | differential_gs | single_arm` plus `mzm_arm_configuration: push_pull | single_arm` so the engine knows how many arms contribute to Vpi*L. Used: `differential: false`.
3. More than two cross-sections per period. The T-rail period here has 4 distinct sections: (A) slot + bars 36 um, (B) slot, gap c 3 um, (C) stem bridge 11 um (no slot, 200 nm metal stem width t = 5 um), (D) stem-free bridge 6 um (3 um each side, pads only, no slot). Propose `loading.cells: [{cross_section: <name>, length_um: <x>}, ...]` replacing `loaded_length_um` / `*_cross_section`, with the optical overlap integral and RLGC averaged by cell length. Used: 2-cut form, loaded (A) 36 um, unloaded (B) 14 um (C and D lumped into B, flagged in limitations).
4. `loaded_cross_section: null` is ambiguous (top-level `geometry`, or invalid). Propose: always name both cuts in `alt_geometries` OR state that null means top-level `geometry`. Used: null meaning top-level geometry.
5. Floating / tied pads. In the 2D cut the T-bars are isolated metal islands tied in 3D to an electrode by a stem outside the plane. Propose electrode roles `tied_signal` / `tied_ground` (or document that `role`/`weight` of an isolated rect is an imposed potential and not a floating conductor). Used: pads as `signal`/`ground` with weight 1/0.
6. Round / arc geometry. Regions allow only `rect` and `polygon_um`; the undercut is an isotropic etch (circle of radius ~35 um). Propose `circle_um: {center: [x, y], radius: r, theta_deg: [a0, a1]}` or `arc` primitive. Used: 19-point half-disc polygons (10 deg steps, polygon chord error < 0.9 um).
7. Silicon conductivity. Substrate loss matters for undercut devices; `materials.silicon` should support `sigma_Sm` (and `tan_delta_rf`) with an explicit rule when absent (engine treats as lossless and warns). Used: left out; listed in `missing`.
8. Bound-type targets. The headline bandwidth is a lower bound (3 dB point not reached below the 67 GHz instrument limit). Propose `{metric: bw3db_ghz, bound: lower, value: 67}` (also `upper`), pass if sim >= value. Used: omitted from `targets`, noted in a comment.
9. Targets not backed by a devices.csv column. The paper's own simulation (VpiL 2.10 V cm, alpha0 0.19, Zc ~52, n_m ~2.16 from Fig. 2) and EO roll-off at given frequencies have no CSV column (evidence entries must map to CSV fields), so `targets[].source` needs an inline form. Used: `source: {paper_id, locator, basis: simulated | extracted_from_figure | derived | measured, note}`. Propose formalising it, and adding `eo_rolloff_db` + `eo_rolloff_at_ghz` (+ optional second point) columns to devices.csv.
10. Variants of one cross-section (5, 7, 10 mm). Propose `variants: [{device_id, line: {length_mm}, targets: [...]}]` so one config covers the length series. Used: config for the 10 mm device only; chen2022-a/-b not covered.
11. Alpha0 law. RF loss is given as alpha0*sqrt(f) (0.36 dB/cm/GHz^0.5). `rf_loss_table` takes tabulated points; propose `line.rf_loss_law: {alpha0_db_per_cm_per_sqrtghz: 0.36}` for from_paper_alpha mode, and a matching devices.csv column.
12. Which sections count for the overlap. State in SPEC that Vpi*L is the length-weighted average over the cell cuts (fed from the `cells` list in item 3). Paper's 2.10 V cm is from the pad cut only.
13. Provenance `locator` allows free text; `note` and `citation` keys are used here for standard_reference and figure_digitized (not in SPEC text). Propose listing `note` as optional.
14. Optical wavelength unknown. SPEC requires `optics.wavelength_nm`; the paper never states it. Used: 1550 as project_inference plus entry in `missing`. Propose allowing `wavelength_nm_assumed: true` or a provenance-driven warning flag the app shows.
