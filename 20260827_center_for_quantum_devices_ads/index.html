Lab context

The tool mix follows the full device stack.

Northwestern describes CQD as a compound-semiconductor and nanotechnology center founded in 1992. Its work spanned III-nitride UV devices, quantum cascade lasers, type-II-superlattice infrared detectors, and electron-beam nanofabrication—hence the unusually complete chain from MBE/MOCVD epitaxy through lithography, etch, deposition, packaging, and optical/electrical test.

Public-status caveat: Northwestern’s current faculty page still identifies Manijeh Razeghi as CQD director. This catalog reflects the supplied August 2026 disposition audit; it does not independently establish the center’s legal or administrative closure.
