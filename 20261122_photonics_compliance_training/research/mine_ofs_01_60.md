## A. "Nothing Is Single-Mode": parasitic modes, radiated power, back-reflections

- Expert population (my estimate): 300-2,000. Judged as: several thousand people do PIC/fiber design, but far fewer treat radiated and non-guided modes as power that has to be accounted for in an energy budget. His own framing: "This is the danger many ignore about optics and photonics, there are always modes around you, nothing is really single-mode."
- Why niche: textbook S-matrix and single-mode models hide the radiation continuum. The failures show up only as unexplained excess loss, ripples or crosstalk.
- Visual potential: high. Code-generated BPM/FDTD toy (he has a BPM simulator, `interactwave`, and says in OFS-60 he wants multi-wavelength and cascaded elements in it). An MMI or Y-combiner driven in-phase vs out-of-phase, with the radiated light drawn as a visible spray. Blender vignette: a waveguide Y-junction leaking like bad plumbing, with an "HR incident report" stamped on the puddle.

## B. Ferroelectric EO crystals: "a nice and messy dielectric" (LN, LT, BTO, KTN, STO)

- Expert population (my estimate): 300-1,500 (TFLN/BTO integrated EO groups and companies, plus bulk LN/LT device engineers). His wording: LN is "a nice and messy dielectrics" (OFS-37).
- Why niche: the problems are crystal-physics side effects (pyroelectricity, domain physics, tensor signs, phase transitions) that only people who have made devices in these materials have run into.
- Visual potential: high. Blender: a LiTaO3 crystal warmed from -30 C to 10 C arcs like a Tesla coil. Interactive: EO-tensor Sankey that updates as the crystal rotates (his own stated next step, OFS-46). Interactive: dielectric constant vs temperature near the Curie point, drawn as a "resonance".

## C. Tunable / narrow-linewidth lasers: mode hops and spec-sheet fine print

- Expert population (my estimate): 200-1,000 (integrated narrow-linewidth/tunable laser groups, ITLA vendors, atomic-physics laser builders).
- Why niche: headline tuning range vs mode-hop-free range, efficiency definitions, and cavity formulas that fail outside their usual regime are known mainly to people who have locked these lasers.
- Visual potential: high. Interactive: longitudinal-mode comb x Vernier ring filter x gain curve as tuning knobs move, with a mode-hop alarm. Blender: a laser line hopping between stepping stones over a pond.

## D. Polarization and reciprocity in fiber systems and detectors

- Expert population (my estimate): 100-800 (FOG designers and winders, deployed quantum-network fiber teams, SNSPD designers, low-birefringence cavity metrologists).
- Why niche: Shupe-type errors and polarization drift are system-level effects that only appear in coiled or deployed fiber, and the standard fix (winding symmetry) is commonly misunderstood. He misread it himself and corrected the post.
- Visual potential: very high. Blender: quadrupolar coil winding from the middle out, half CW and half CCW. Interactive: heat front moving through a coil while CW/CCW pulses pass different points at different times, with a phase readout that looks like rotation. Interactive: Poincare-sphere random walk for deployed fiber.
