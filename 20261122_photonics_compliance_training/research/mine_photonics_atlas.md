# Photonics-atlas mining: niche pitfall clusters for the compliance-course parody

Date: 2026-11-22. Corpus: `photonics-atlas` (read-only). Text extracts: `references/<paper_id>/text.md` (page-marked `<!-- page N -->`, PyMuPDF text; many are paywalled `restricted_local_only` - cite the paper, never redistribute the extract). Metadata: `references/<paper_id>/source.json`. Component rows: `data/components/components.csv`. Locators below are `references/<id>/text.md p.N` (all paths relative to the photonics-atlas repo root).

Method: keyword-in-context sweep over 1686 text extracts (reference lists stripped), then manual reading of the hits; per-paper component classes from `components.csv`. Every pitfall row was checked against the extract text at the stated page. Columns "why experts miss it" and "gag/visual" are my own inference throughout. Confidence: `stated` = the paper says it; `stated+inf` = paper states the mechanism/observation, my sentence generalizes it into the pitfall framing; `inf` = my inference.
