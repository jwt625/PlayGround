## Drawing rules derived

- Flip-chip (HHI photo): the InP die is p-side down, so from the top you see only its backside (gold n-metal, textured), not the ridge or the p-pad. Electrical access is gold traces on the host chip running out from under the die to probe pads; no wire bond on the die in that photo.
- Butt or edge coupling: the laser output facet faces the chip input waveguide. Waveguides at the interface may be angled (Franken: "angled to mitigate Fresnel reflections"; angle in degrees N/A), so draw the waveguide meeting the facet off-normal, not straight on.
- Photonic wire bonds are 3D arcs (bend radius >60 um, tapers >100 um each), drawn as a loop between the two chip facets; electrical wire bonds sit separately on the pads.
- Butterfly: 7 pins per long side (14 total), fiber leaves axially from one short end through a round snout and boot; 4 flange holes at the corners. Pins 1 and 14 are at the fiber end (SemiNex drawing). Laser anode/cathode on pins 10/11, TEC on 1/14, thermistor on 2/5 (Type 1).
- Inline isolator: a plain cylinder (5.5 mm dia x 35 mm, or 3.0 x 25 mm mini) with one fiber out each end and a single arrow printed along the body. Convention (not stated on the pages read): the arrow points in the forward direction, input fiber to output fiber.
- Ring heaters: either a metal trace on top of the cladding following the ring (spiral Ti, 1 um wide, over ~1.2 um of oxide/HSQ) or a buried metal under the ring; the heater does not cover the bus coupling region (Moille). Leads route away from the bus to pads; rings are spaced far apart (150 um in Shen) to limit thermal crosstalk.
- Grating coupler: teeth period ~630 nm on 220 nm SOI (shallow etch, 50% fill); a non-focusing GC needs a 300-500 um linear taper, a focusing GC only tens of um. 2D GC is a ~10 x 10 um square hole array.
- Fiber array above GCs: 127 um pitch, fibers tilted ~8-12 deg from vertical.
- MMI 1x2: a rectangle ~2.8 um wide x 27 um long on SOI (Wang), much longer than wide.
- Lensed fiber: tip is a near-hemispherical/hyperbolic cap of radius ~3-13 um on a cone of ~80-88 deg, spot (MFD) ~2-6 um, facing an inverse taper whose tip is 160-480 nm wide at the chip facet.
- Ring backscatter doublet: draw two dips separated by about one linewidth (118 MHz splitting vs ~110 MHz loaded linewidth in Raja), not two fully resolved narrow dips.
- OSA trace: y axis in dB (or dBm), x in nm; state RBW (20 pm used in Franken). DFB main peak with side peaks ~45-60 dB down.
