Objective and training scope

Mean over 120 steps: normalized target intensity + 0.25 × PIB − 0.1 × pointing error (rad) − 0.001 × mean squared piston change. PIB bucket: 1 mrad. Evolution strategies train 1,235 readout parameters; connectivity is fixed. Analytic steering assistance. Initial/final policies only; intermediate policies were not saved.
