# ETH Zurich / Leuthold / Polariton lineage — initial research memo

## Decision supported

This tranche establishes which person-level paths can defensibly connect Jürg Leuthold's Bell Labs work, his KIT and ETH groups, Polariton's founding team, and later integrated-electro-optics leadership. It also separates the technology stack into active plasmonics, silicon-photonics integration, and unsupported TFLN claims.

## Bottom line

- The dataset contains **20 named people, 14 sources, 1 acquisition event, and 37 validated edges**. All rendered edges use A/B evidence.
- Leuthold's backbone is directly supported: ETH doctorate (1998), Lucent Bell Labs device/system research (1999–2004), KIT institute leadership (2004–2013), and ETH IEF leadership from 2013.
- Polariton is a formally identified 2019 spin-off from Leuthold's IEF, founded by Claudia Hössbacher, Wolfgang Heni, and Benedikt Bäuerle. A first-person interview places all three with Leuthold at KIT at the master's stage and then in Zurich; Claudia's company bio separately gives her 2017 ETH doctorate and Leuthold-group postdoc.
- Polariton's core distinction is **plasmonic active electro-optic devices integrated with silicon-photonics platforms**. Reviewed sources do not support treating the company as TFLN. Because the schema lacks a `plasmonics` technology term, records use `silicon_photonics` for the integration platform and preserve plasmonic/organic-hybrid specificity in notes and roles.
- Downstream paths are individually evidenced for Christian Haffner (Leuthold-group PhD → imec electro-optics leadership), Maurizio Burla (IEF → TU Berlin photonics chair), and Yannick Salamin (Leuthold-supervised doctorate → MIT postdoc → UCF CREOL; only the ETH and UCF edges are represented here). These are not described as company spinouts.
- Marvell's April 2026 acquisition is a corporate event. No person-to-Marvell or team-transfer edge is created because the release's team language names no individuals.
