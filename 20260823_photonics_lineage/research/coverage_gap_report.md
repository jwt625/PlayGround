# Release coverage gap report

## Executive result

Audit of **88 major company/startup/lab seeds** against 117 organizations, 240 people, 223 sources, 13 events, and 424 canonical edges.

- Exact nodes: **60/88**; parent-only groups: **7**; missing nodes: **21**.
- Company seeds with A/B founder coverage: **26/54**; with A/B CTO/technical leadership: **12/54**.
- Planned acquisitions present: **10/20**; 10 have announcement dates, 8 effective dates, and 8 headline values.

The principal release risk is unevenness: recent acquisition-first targets and researched academic lineages are represented much better than historical acquisitions, current startups, China company nodes, and exact corporate research-group nodes.

## Reproducibility

The companion YAML stores SHA-256 hashes for all canonical inputs, the complete seed manifest, formulas, per-seed counts, and ranked backlog. Counts use only validated A/B edges. Organization presence never substitutes for founder or technical-leadership evidence.
