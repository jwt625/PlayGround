## 4. Logged audit items: judgment and convention

- Rank as a numeric value (ectc.2013.6575597-c-m1..m6, unit 'rank', from Fig. 13 with no axis values; the ranking is
  the extractor's own reading). Judgment: wrong home. The numeric `value` must stay a physical quantity, count or ratio,
  so that aggregations never treat an ordinal as a magnitude. Convention:
  - Keep `value` empty.
  - Put the order in `value_text`, either per variant ("rank 3 of 6 by earliest first failure") or once
    ("first-failure order: A < B < ...").
  - Set source figure_image and add the note "ordinal reading, no axis values".
  - In v2.1, add `value_kind` (quantity | ratio | count | ordinal | pass_fail | text).
  - The validator rejects `value` when value_kind = ordinal.
- Unattributed claims copied onto several TVs (00175 "<100 um" on mTV1 and mTV2; DB-wide 23 groups).
  Judgment: copying inflates counts and falsely attributes a result to each build. Convention:
  - A paper-level claim with no build named goes once on the paper's pooled TV.
  - A claim the paper states for all builds ("for all process options") goes once, with `applies_to: [tv ids]`.
  - Per-build results stay per build.
  - The validator warns on identical (parameter, value, value_text, unit, variant, locator) across sibling TVs.
  - Fix the 4 confirmed copy groups in section 3 item 4.
- Pooled TV (00052-a, tv_kind other, used for Fig. 9-10 data from single-PIC and 3-PIC modules). Judgment: correct
  instinct, wrong enum. Convention:
  - tv_kind `pooled`, tv_id `<paper_id>-p`.
  - Required `member_tv_ids`; package_class = the members' class.
  - Only metrics and process_steps may hang off it (no components, stack_layers or interconnects).
  - Per-build analyses exclude it by tv_kind.
  - The same mechanism carries paper-level claims (previous bullet).

## 5. Recommended schema v2.1 changes (top 10, by impact on stack-up and process-flow reproduction)

1. metrics statistics: value_min, value_max, value_sd, value_q1, value_q3, value_kind (see section 4).
   This moves the 131 numbers stuck in value_text and fixes ranges, box-plot reads and ranks.
2. TV model:
   - tv_kind += pooled, design_concept.
   - member_tv_ids (pooled); applies_to (list) on metrics/process_steps.
   - parent_tv_id / sweep_of for DOE legs that change the stack (b015).
   - substrate_area_mm2 on test_vehicles (00033 TV-1/2 give only area).
3. Interconnect geometry:
   - diameter_bottom_um for tapered TGV/TSV.
   - cd_x_um and cd_y_um for non-circular vias (90x120 nm).
   - pad_diameter_um vs opening_diameter_um; capture_pad_um/enclosure_um.
   - *_min/*_max for diameter and height.
   - count_scope enum (per_chain, per_die, per_package, total). Count semantics differ today:
     180 per chain (00274), 3,000,000 total (00166), 300 per chain (00179).
4. stack_layers: line_pitch_um (when only pitch is given: BPR 105 nm, 1 um routing pitch) and pad_pitch_um, distinct from
   via_pitch_um; plus a layer-level `variant` for material/thickness splits on one TV.
5. Taxonomy enums (each hit in 2+ batches):
   - ic_type += tpv, tiv, cu_post (through-mold/stud), conductive_paste_via, hybrid_unspecified_metal, dielectric_bond.
   - level += die_to_fanout_rdl, die_to_wafer (non-hybrid), package_on_package.
   - bonding_method += fluxless_reflow, tcb_ncp, electroless_plating_bond, fusion_d2w.
   - step_category += plating (generic), bump_formation, laser_modification, drilling.
   - material_class += compound_semiconductor.
   - component_type += temporary_carrier, release_layer, waveguide, rf_ic, fluid_manifold.
6. Process steps:
   - force_n; tack vs anneal pressure; atmosphere.
   - flow_id with depends_on, for parallel flows (TSV-mid vs bump vs reveal) and shared flows reused across TVs
     (00244 copies a 23-step flow 3x).
   - metrics.stage (after Via1 cure, post top grind) to tie a measurement to a step.
7. Provenance fields:
   - basis/override value `inferred`.
   - `attributed_source` (external tables like DECA/Yole).
   - papers.text_layer_status (clean | garbled_ocr | numbers_garbled) and pages_ocr.
   - figure_axis_issue as a conflict type.
8. Optical:
   - link_loss_db separate from coupling_loss_db.
   - waveguide_pitch_um separate from fiber_pitch_um.
   - wavelength_min_nm/max_nm, alignment_tolerance_um, statistic, n_samples.
   - crosstalk_db with a sign convention.
9. Lid/heat spreader and carrier geometry:
   - foot_width_mm, outer_x/y_mm, lid_thickness via component thickness.
   - carrier hierarchy (sub-panel within panel, b019).
   - warpage_sign enum on metrics (cry/smile, +/- with reference side).
10. Controlled vocabulary and validator rules:
    - unit list (Ohm, mOhm, a.u., x_vs_ref) with optional value_si.
    - qualifier required when value_text/notes contains "<", "less than", "~", "about" or "up to".
    - ic_type/pitch sanity (c4 below 80 um, microbump above 100 um: warn).
    - HB level/bonding_method pairing.
    - sibling-TV duplicate warning.
    - numeric process conditions basis = design_target.
