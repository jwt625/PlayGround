## Deliberate illustration changes

The generated NVIDIA, Meta, xAI, Stargate and Anthropic plates may include small company/site signs to make the meme readable. Their placement is illustrative. In particular, the old Slack wordmark is replaced by ANTHROPIC. No claim is made that those signs exist at those exact locations. All people are removed; colors become low-contrast grayscale. The preserved architecture, not invented city infrastructure, supplies the scene identity.
