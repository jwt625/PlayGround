# Casting notes

These are selective narrative and visual parallels, not claims that the real people share GTA characters' criminal acts or full biographies. All on-screen dialogue is written for this meme. This first test uses recognizable figures and distinct props rather than trying to cast every character shown in the cached montage.

| AI figure | GTA IV counterpart | Match and limits | Prop / dialogue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ilya Sutskever | Niko Bellic | **Medium:** newcomer/restarting protagonist with a singular mission. SSI provides a real fresh-start/single-purpose anchor; Niko's war history and revenge plot do not transfer. Brown striped jacket supplies the visual cue. | Research notebook and hard case. “I came here for safe superintelligence.” |
| Sam Altman | Roman Bellic | **Strong comic structure:** the optimistic contact promising the big opportunity, summoning the protagonist into a new world. Compute/infrastructure ambitions replace the American-dream pitch. This does not import Roman's debts, gambling, or family relationship. | Phone and data-center plans. “Cousin! Let’s build another gigawatt.” |
| Jensen Huang | Little Jacob | **Strong functional match:** the contact who supplies the equipment everyone else needs. The hardware seller becomes the GPU supplier. Not a visual or biographical match to Jacob. | Leather jacket, glasses, green accelerator board. “I got the hardware. You got the power?” |
| Elon Musk | Brucie Kibbutz | **Medium, persona-driven:** conspicuous machines, competitive performance, and constant escalation. Colossus gives the compute angle a concrete anchor. Brucie's steroid use is not part of this parody. | Rocket model and accelerator module. “More compute. More power. More everything.” |
| Dario Amodei | United Liberty Paper contact | **Loose:** serious institutional briefing, rules, and the next mission; safety/governance translated into the handler's visual role. This does not suggest intelligence-agency employment. This is the weakest biographical match. | Policy dossier and reading glasses. “One more mission. First, the safety eval.” |
| Mark Zuckerberg | Playboy X | **Loose:** ambitious young empire-builder, status, and a self-authored vision of the future. Blue clothes and chain connect the visual language; the llama supplies the AI-specific joke. No betrayal subplot is transferred. | Llama plush and laptop. “The weights are open. The empire is mine.” |

The Sam/Roman and Jensen/Jacob jokes are the clearest at a glance. Ilya/Niko is the central story choice. Dario and Mark are useful visual variety, but should not be sold as close backstory equivalents. A future pass could substitute other figures after deciding whether the desired joke is about lab schisms, hardware dependency, or founder personas.

## Evidence behind the joke

These sources anchor the real-world motifs; the casting itself is editorial interpretation. Historical announcements are used for recognizable themes, not current numerical claims.

- [SSI mission](https://ssi.inc/): single-purpose safe-superintelligence focus.
- [OpenAI's Stargate announcement, January 2025](https://openai.com/index/announcing-the-stargate-project/): infrastructure ambition behind Sam's plans/phone/gigawatt joke. Announced investment intentions are not treated as completed buildout.
- [NVIDIA on the original Colossus deployment, October 2024](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/spectrum-x-ethernet-networking-xai-colossus): the GPU supply and compute-scale motifs for Jensen and Elon.
- [Anthropic's leadership page](https://www.anthropic.com/company/leadership) and [Dario's AI Safety Summit remarks](https://www.anthropic.com/news/uk-ai-safety-summit): identity and responsible-scaling motif for the dossier.
- [Zuckerberg's “Open Source AI Is the Path Forward,” July 2024](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/07/open-source-ai-is-the-path-forward/amp/): Llama/open-model motif. The meme's “weights are open” is an invented line, not a quote or a claim about every Meta model or license.
- [GTA IV character summaries](https://www.gta4.net/characters/), [Little Jacob](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Little_Jacob), [Playboy X](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Playboy), and [United Liberty Paper contact](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/United_Liberty_Paper_contact): secondary references for game roles.

## Optional hand-drawing refinement

The editable PNGs already work as cutouts. To improve likeness without rebuilding the animation, replace one asset with a transparent portrait of the same 2:3 aspect ratio. Keep the head, hands, and carried prop inside the edges; crop the body below mid-thigh. Favor broad ink contours and 3–5 hard shadow planes rather than soft photo shading. Keep pose rigid; motion comes from the renderer. Ilya and Dario would benefit most from reference-photo-guided face refinement.
