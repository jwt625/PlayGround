## Summary

- Checks logged: 40 rows. CONFIRMED (incl. partial): 27; WRONG or internally contradicted (incl. the WRONG half of split verdicts): 7; MISLABELED accuracy level: 3; UNVERIFIED: 6.
- Spec-derived interconnect numbers (OSFP, QSFP-DD, OSFP cage, ELSFP) are accurate against the primary drawings. Real problems are in cross-asset consistency and in model envelopes that exceed the A-level source they cite.
- Not run: Blender (bbox values were taken from the JSON), logos in baked image textures (only scripts and JSON were searched), CM300 photo-scale estimates (C).

## Consistency findings

1. **XPU OE site vs oe_module_cpo (WRONG).** xpu_package_rubin_style hooks assume 14 x 10 mm sites at 11.5 mm pitch, 8 per side along a 92 mm lid span. oe_module_cpo is a 24 x 20 mm carrier with an 18.5 x 16 mm lid. The pitch direction is y: a 20 mm (or even 16 mm lid) module cannot sit at 11.5 mm pitch, and 8 x 20 = 160 mm does not fit the 92 mm lid span (about 4 modules per side would). In x the 24 mm carrier centred at |x| = 81 mm spans 69..93 mm: it overlaps the lid frame (outer x = 71 mm) and overhangs the 90 mm package half-width by 3 mm. Packaging devlog open item 1 is still unresolved. Fix by deciding the OE module size first, then recomputing site count, pitch and |x|.
2. **rack_pair ports vs 14-cable bundle (OK with notes).** 14 glands (2 rows x 7, z 1.35 / 1.70 m) match the 14-cable bundle count; 28 port hooks = 2 x 14. The bundle hooks default to z = 1.0 m (wall asset), the rack_pair ports sit at 1.35 / 1.70 m, so the assembler must retarget the bundle ends. Glands are 60 mm for a bundle of 14 cables at 7.5 mm OD (about 33 mm bundle envelope): fine physically; individual cables are not routed per gland.
3. **OSFP module vs cage (OK).** Module 22.58 x 13.00 against cage internal 22.88 x 13.30; envelope 22.93 x (13.0 + 1.6 = 14.6) against bezel cut-out 24.28 x 14.70: all fit with spec clearances. Cage length 91.0 is marked B (reference).
4. **Bench scope and DCA screens vs eye textures (OK).** Eye frames are 384 x 240 (1.60); bench screen 256 x 160 (1.60), DCA 216 x 135 (1.60). No stretch.
5. **HGX/OAM vs xpu package (OK/B).** OAM 102 x 165 mm and 103 mm pitch consistent with the OCP spec; 2 x 165 + 119 + 99 = 548 mm fits 553 mm. The S4 socket (200 x 120 outer, 180.4 x 100.4 inner) fits the 180 x 100 mm XPU package with 0.2 mm clearance. The Rubin-style package is not an OAM and the HGX board carries OAM-sized sites: acceptable for a style asset but not stated in the JSON.
6. **Chip sizes vs footprints (OK).** Retimer 15 mm body, 17 x 17 array at 0.8 mm (12.8 mm span); LGA/FCBGA 45 mm body with 43 x 43 at 1.0 mm (42 mm span). Interposer 120 x 62 holds 4 x 26 + 2 + 8 = 114 mm and 33 + 2 x (1.5 + 11) = 58 mm.
7. **NVL72 cartridge vs bay (WRONG)**: see check 14. **Gary stature (WRONG label)**: see check 37.
8. **Envelope vs A-level source:** probe station height +70 mm, dicing saw +520 mm wide / +406 mm tall, FOUP +9 mm tall / +13 mm deep, HGX +3.1 mm in Y. Either keep the extra geometry (signal tower, hoods) and say so in the JSON, or trim it.

## Logos and trademarks

Searched scripts/assets/**/*.py and all assets/components JSON for brand names (NVIDIA, Intel, Broadcom, Keysight, Tektronix, FormFactor, Cascade, Molex, Ciena, Nubis, Cisco, Disco, Amphenol, Supermicro, Ranovus, Marvell, Innolight and others) and for every string passed to text_mesh/label/labels helpers.

- **No real company logo or real brand wordmark was found as geometry** on any asset. Brand names appear only in source/provenance strings, docstrings and JSON `sources`.
- Parody marks present as intended: MOLEXX, NUBISS, TERAHOP, AYARR, NVYDIA, OPENAY, ANTHROPY on shirts; NARROWCOM on the retimer chip, EVB silk, PIC die, OE-16 and NPO-16 module marking.
- **Borderline: real product-line names used as marking text on models** (spec says real names are for docs and metadata only):
  - probe_station_cm300_style: text meshes "CM300-STYLE" (label_right_1, build_probe_station_cm300_style.py line 134), "ULN" (line 135) and "VUE-STYLE" (line 228). CM300xi, ULN and (Cascade) names are FormFactor product naming; "-STYLE" does not make them neutral. The JSON claims 'no vendor logos' but does not list these as exceptions. Recommend generic "WAFER PROBE" only.
  - hgx_baseboard: silk text "HGX-STYLE GPU BASEBOARD 553 X 416" (line 321); HGX is an NVIDIA product name. Recommend "GPU BASEBOARD 553 X 416".
  - Form-factor standard names printed on labels ("OSFP", "QSFP-DD", "GENERIC 800G DR8"): standards names, acceptable. A QSFP-DD MSA string appears only in build_aoc_whip.py source comments.
- NVYDIA shirt colour (#76c21a, green) evokes the real brand colour; the wordmark itself is the parody. Low risk; mention for the film's legal review.
- Not checked: pixels of baked/generated textures (assets/generated_textures, packed images) and any text inside the previews.
