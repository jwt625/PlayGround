## 4. Open questions

1. TIA die size, inductor sizes and pad pitch of real octal TIAs are not public (N/A); the model is a plausible SiGe-class floorplan, not a copy of any product.
2. The owner's 400G/800G photos show 4-ch RX blocks with a glass turning block over the PDs; the 2x4 -> 1x8 interleaver is an invention for the fiber-free eye.
3. 25 um wire diameter and 65 um loop height are typical values, not measured from the photos.
