# Research: bird facts for the pigeon-pluggable mapping

Date: 2026-10-09. Method: research subagent, web sources, read-only. Confidence per row. Items marked N/A were not fetched.

## Phylogeny: where trees disagree

- Jarvis et al. 2014: Neoaves split into Columbea (flamingos + grebes + pigeons/sandgrouse/mesites) and Passerea.
- Prum et al. 2015: Strisores (nightjars, swifts, hummingbirds) diverge first, then Columbaves.
- Stiller et al. 2024 (Nature, family-level genomes, PMC11111414): Mirandornithes (flamingos + grebes) sister to all other Neoaves, but weak (bootstrap 64 in concatenated tree; exon-only tree places them differently). Columbaves = Columbimorphae (pigeons, sandgrouse, mesites) + Otidimorphae (cuckoos, bustards, turacos). Strisores sits inside a new clade Elementaves. Jarvis' Columbea is attributed to a ~21 Mb chromosome-4 outlier region (Mirarab et al. 2024).
- Decision for the film: use Stiller 2024 / Prum 2015 topology where they agree (Palaeognathae / Galloanserae / Neoaves; Columbaves includes cuckoos; Strisores = swifts + hummingbirds + nightjars).

## Safe on screen

- Pigeons see four color channels, one violet beyond human vision.
- Each color cone carries a colored oil droplet acting as a long-pass filter.
- The dodo was a pigeon; closest living relative the Nicobar pigeon.
- Common swifts stay airborne about 10 months.
- 2001-04-28, Bergen: IP over pigeon, 9 pings, 4 replies, 55% loss, ~1.5 h round trip.

## Do not say

- Pigeons have UV cones (shortwave cone is violet, ~409 nm).
- The pigeon compass is solved / beak magnetite.
- Cuckoos are pigeons' closest relatives in all trees; Archaeopteryx is the ancestor of all birds.
